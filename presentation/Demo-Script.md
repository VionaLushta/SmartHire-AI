# SmartHire AI Demo Script

## Demo Goal

Show the end-to-end candidate and recruiter journey without changing the underlying application behavior.

## Opening

Say:

> SmartHire AI is a recruitment platform that combines candidate intake, OCR, AI matching, recruiter review, and analytics in one workflow.

## 1. Landing Page

- Open the landing page.
- Point out the platform purpose, key value proposition, and major user paths.
- Mention that the interface is responsive and role-aware.

## 2. Candidate Registration

- Open the registration form.
- Enter a valid candidate profile.
- Explain that validation protects against malformed input.
- Submit and show the authenticated session flow.

## 3. Resume Upload

- Navigate to the candidate workspace.
- Upload a supported resume file.
- Explain the upload validation checks and size limits.

## 4. OCR

- Show that uploaded documents are converted into usable text.
- Explain that PDF text extraction is used when available and OCR is used as a fallback for image content.

## 5. AI Matching

- Move to the resume or job analysis section.
- Explain how the system compares candidate text to job requirements.
- Highlight skills, similarity scoring, and explanation output.

## 6. Recruiter Dashboard

- Switch to the recruiter or company dashboard.
- Open a candidate application record.
- Explain that recruiter actions are protected by role-based access control.

## 7. Decision

- Choose a recruiter decision such as interview, hold, accept, or reject.
- Explain that the decision updates workflow state and downstream outputs.

## 8. PDF Generation

- Show the generated document or report.
- Explain that the PDF generator creates formal hiring letters and workflow documents.

## 9. Email

- Explain that the email service sends the workflow result and any attachment through SMTP.
- Mention that email templates keep messaging consistent.

## 10. Analytics

- Open the analytics dashboard.
- Show the updated counters, charts, and summary views.
- Explain that workflow events feed reporting views.

## 11. Power BI

- Explain how exported data can be used in Power BI.
- Mention CSV, JSON, and PostgreSQL as reporting inputs.

## Closing

Say:

> SmartHire AI improves hiring speed, consistency, and visibility while keeping security, validation, and workflow control at the center of the platform.
