# SmartHire AI Interview Questions

## Why FastAPI?

FastAPI fits SmartHire AI because it is fast, type-driven, and works well for structured APIs, validation, and service-oriented backend design.

## Why React?

React supports reusable UI components, route-based pages, protected views, and a scalable dashboard experience.

## How does OCR work?

The backend validates uploaded files first, then extracts text from PDFs directly or uses OCR for image-based inputs. The extracted text is normalized before further processing.

## How does NLP work?

NLP preprocessing cleans and normalizes resume and job text. TF-IDF and cosine similarity are used to compare content and produce match scores.

## Why TF-IDF?

TF-IDF is lightweight, explainable, and effective for comparing resumes against job descriptions without requiring a heavy model for the core matching flow.

## How does Power BI integrate?

The backend prepares exports and structured analytics outputs that can be consumed by Power BI through CSV, JSON, or PostgreSQL-based reporting paths.

## Why JWT?

JWT keeps authentication stateless, supports access and refresh tokens, and works well with protected frontend routes and API dependencies.

## How is security handled?

Security is handled through JWT validation, role permissions, request validation, upload restrictions, CORS configuration, and security event logging.

## What was the biggest challenge?

The hardest part was coordinating multiple flows, such as OCR, NLP, workflow automation, PDF generation, and analytics, while keeping the codebase modular and testable.

## What future improvements would you add?

I would add LLM-assisted resume analysis, interview scheduling, calendar integration, SMS notifications, multi-language parsing, and cloud-native deployment support.
